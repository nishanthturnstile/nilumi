# ADR-032 — Encrypted backup and forget-safe restoration

> **Decision status:** Accepted current baseline, 2026-10-10.
> **Delivery status:** planned for MVP; P1/P4/P8. Acceptance of a decision does not establish implemented or production-validated behavior.

## Context

The voice-first PWA uses one dynamic Home canvas and persistent activities. This record owns the encrypted backup and forget-safe restoration choice within that experience.

## Decision

Run nightly 02:30 IST worker backups: drain forget journal, dump, restore-test scratch plaintext, encrypt to public age recipient, upload R2 and verify hash. Worker holds no private decrypt key. Redaction/tombstone/outbox are atomic; upload waits for journal watermark, every restore replays tombstones before traffic.

## Consequences and validation scope

Keep 30 daily/six monthly encrypted backups. Recovery material and runbook are in a dedicated OneDrive document shared with the other adult with 2FA accounts. RPO≤24h/RTO≤2h; quarterly manual master-key restore, including household participation. Activity/presentation deletion, unshare/visibility restriction and device/member revocation extend the content-free journal as their capabilities ship. Replay the latest restrictive lifecycle entries before restoring access; an old backup cannot re-enable a deleted draft, withdrawn share or revoked session. Activity/presentation references are restored only after that membership/visibility/tombstone reconciliation. Existing S5 proof is scoped; production recovery still needs rehearsal.
