# ADR-032 — Encrypted backups, forget-journal recovery and shared recovery material

> **Status:** Accepted
> **Baseline:** Revision 2, reviewed 6 Oct 2026; individual decision date not recorded
> **Origin:** Consolidated from the existing D/Q review and applied revision-2 requirements; no new product decision
> **Legacy references:** D16, Q11

## Context

Family memory must survive host loss and builder absence without resurrecting forgotten content. The review identified a race between forget and backup upload.

## Decision

Run worker-based nightly backups at 02:30 IST: drain the forget journal, dump, restore-test plaintext in scratch, encrypt with the public age recipient, upload to R2 and verify the hash. The worker holds only the public key. Keep the master private key and runbook in one dedicated OneDrive document in a folder shared with the wife; both Microsoft accounts use 2FA. Redaction, tombstone and outbox commit together; backup upload waits for the journal watermark, and every restore replays the journal before traffic.

## Alternatives

Revision-1 Vercel Cron/GitHub Actions backups are superseded by worker cron. A private decrypt/drill key on Railway or GitHub, soft-delete-only recovery and untested dumps are rejected. Railway volume backups alone are insufficient; use them as the second layer.

## Consequences

Retain 30 daily and six monthly encrypted backups. Old backups can contain later-forgotten content until expiry, disclosed in Privacy. Scratch plaintext exists only for the job and is deleted; `fa_maint` needs `CREATEDB`. Quarterly manual master-key restore tests the human path, ideally once with the wife. RPO ≤ 24 h and RTO ≤ 2 h. Decryption needs both recovery material and the R2 bucket.

Create and walk through the shared recovery document before the S5 manual restore. Restore proof, volume backup support and optional PITR remain S5 gates; full operational steps and runbook contents remain in Architecture.
