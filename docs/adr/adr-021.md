# ADR-021 — Vercel: free open-source libraries only (Next.js, Turbopack, AI SDK); no metered Vercel platform services

> **Status:** Accepted — amended by [ADR-046](adr-046.md) (October 8, 2026): Vercel AI Gateway credits are the one metered Vercel service exception
> **Baseline:** Revision 2, reviewed 6 Oct 2026; individual decision date not recorded
> **Origin:** Extracted from Architecture §21; existing ID and accepted meaning retained
> **Legacy references:** D19
> **Supersedes / superseded by:** Amended by [ADR-046](adr-046.md)

## Context

The owner prioritizes low recurring cost, simplicity and freedom from metered platform dependencies.

The ecosystem inventory distinguishes free libraries from hosted services. Workflow runtime portability is incomplete and its Postgres World was not production-hardened in the reviewed evidence. Revision-1 hosted choices are superseded by revision 2, without allocating retrospective ADR IDs.

## Decision

use Vercel-authored open-source libraries where useful, but do not use metered or platform-bound services.

## Alternatives

Vercel Hobby hosting + Workflow + Cron + AI Gateway from revision 1.

## Consequences

no free-tier cliffs or platform lock-in; jobs, realtime and backups stay in Postgres + worker; Railway owns hosting.

Optional copy-in AI Elements remain allowed. This rule applies to Vercel, not a new prohibition on every paid processor.

## October 8 amendment — ADR-046

[ADR-038](adr-038.md) routed LLM and embedding calls through Vercel AI Gateway
without recording an exception here. [ADR-046](adr-046.md) records it:
- **Exception.** Vercel AI Gateway, funded by purchased credits with auto top-up off, is the only metered Vercel service allowed.
- **Still excluded.** Hosting, Workflow, Cron, Blob, KV and other platform services; Railway still hosts everything.
- **Paid plans.** A Vercel Pro or Enterprise plan is a fixed platform subscription. It needs its own decision and cost at the production privacy gate.
