# ADR-029 — Household date and time defaults

> **Decision status:** Accepted current baseline, 2026-10-10.
> **Delivery status:** planned for MVP; P5. Acceptance of a decision does not establish implemented or production-validated behavior.

## Context

The voice-first PWA uses one dynamic Home canvas and persistent activities. This record owns the household date and time defaults choice within that experience.

## Decision

Resolve relative dates at the turn occurred_at in Asia/Kolkata. Editable defaults: morning 09:00, afternoon 14:00, evening 18:30, tonight 20:30, weekend Saturday 10:00. Show concrete dates/times; day-first parsing and deterministic checks constrain model interpretation.

## Consequences and validation scope

Ambiguous/past time, partial parse or disagreement requires clarification. Store precision and recurrence; preserve confirmed absolute times on retries. Travel/per-member time zones are deferred.
