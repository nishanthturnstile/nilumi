# ADR-029 — Household timezone and reminder time defaults

> **Status:** Accepted
> **Baseline:** Revision 2, reviewed 6 Oct 2026; individual decision date not recorded
> **Origin:** Consolidated from the existing D/Q review and applied revision-2 requirements; no new product decision
> **Legacy references:** Q4

## Context

Vague day parts and relative dates need consistent, inspectable resolution for an Indian household.

## Decision

Use `Asia/Kolkata` and resolve relative expressions against the turn's `occurred_at`. Keep editable defaults: morning 09:00, afternoon 14:00, evening 18:30, tonight 20:30, weekend Saturday 10:00. Show the concrete time on cards. Use day-first parsing and deterministic cross-checks of LLM resolution.

## Alternatives

The accepted review kept the defaults rather than reopening them. Trusting LLM dates alone is rejected; travel and per-member timezones are outside MVP scope.

## Consequences

Disagreements and ambiguous/past reminder times need clarification. Precision and recurrence remain stored explicitly; implementation edge rules and libraries belong to Architecture and Tech Stack.

Q4 kept the existing morning/evening/tonight defaults. Other defaults are consolidated from the same Architecture baseline; this extraction adds no new time policy.
