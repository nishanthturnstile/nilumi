# ADR-038 — LLM and embeddings route through Vercel AI Gateway with team-wide zero-retention and no-prompt-training enforcement

> **Status:** Accepted — partially superseded by [ADR-046](adr-046.md) (October 8, 2026): ZDR and the dashboard allowlist move to the production privacy gate; Vercel AI Gateway routing, no-training enforcement and the registry remain
> **Baseline:** October 2026
> **Origin:** Owner decision during S0 (provider eligibility)
> **Legacy references:** None
> **Supersedes / superseded by:** Supersedes the "no hosted gateway" part of [ADR-022](adr-022.md); partially superseded by [ADR-046](adr-046.md)

## Context

Nilumi sends transcripts (family conversation text) to LLM and embedding providers. The previous baseline (ADR-022) used direct SDK calls with the household's own keys, rejecting AI Gateway as metered and platform-bound. Vercel has since added team-wide Zero Data Retention (prompts/responses permanently deleted after each request), a `disallowPromptTraining` enforcement flag (routing only to providers with verified no-training agreements), and provider/model allowlists. Routing LLM/embeddings traffic through AI Gateway gives a single, enforceable data-privacy control point instead of per-provider dashboard settings. STT/TTS (Sarvam, ElevenLabs) and all hosting/data services stay direct — no gateway covers that audio path.

## Decision

LLM and embeddings calls go through Vercel AI Gateway with team-wide Zero Data Retention and `disallowPromptTraining` enabled and a provider allowlist limited to approved models. The in-process provider registry with model-role aliases (ADR-022) and the fallback wrapper remain; they target AI Gateway model IDs instead of direct provider endpoints. Sarvam STT/TTS and ElevenLabs remain direct adapters.

## Alternatives

Direct OpenAI/Anthropic SDK calls (rejected: privacy controls scattered across provider dashboards, no single enforcement point); OpenRouter/Portkey (fees, continuity concerns recorded in ADR-022); Cloudflare AI Gateway (another processor without decisive advantage — retained as evidence in Research).

## Consequences

Vercel becomes a processor of family transcript text and is recorded as such in Tech §7; its ZDR default, no-training enforcement and allowlist must stay enabled — a regression fails the privacy gate. LLM/embedding spend goes through Vercel's billing, which changes the exact price capture in `config/models.ts`. Sarvam and ElevenLabs terms remain independent of this decision.

## October 8 amendment — ADR-046

The October 7 verification showed that Hobby cannot enable team-wide ZDR or
dashboard allowlists. [ADR-046](adr-046.md) replaces these operative clauses:

- **"Team-wide Zero Data Retention … enabled."** During the founding-household validation pilot, ZDR is not required. At the production privacy gate, ZDR is required on every LLM and embedding request, either per request or team-wide.
- **"A provider allowlist limited to approved models."** This becomes a per-request `only` list set by one gateway wrapper and checked against routing receipts. Dashboard allowlists are added when the plan supports them.
- **Consequences: "ZDR default, no-training enforcement and allowlist must stay enabled."** The controls in force for the current stage must stay enabled; a regression fails the privacy gate:
  - **Pilot:** no-training, the `only` list, `store: false` where supported, and the no-BYOK rule.
  - **Production:** the pilot controls plus ZDR.

Routing through Vercel AI Gateway, `disallowPromptTraining`, the role registry
and the direct STT/TTS adapters remain as decided here.
