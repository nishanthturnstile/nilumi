# ADR-031 — Default memory visibility and private-item existence protection

> **Status:** Accepted
> **Baseline:** Revision 2, reviewed 6 Oct 2026; individual decision date not recorded
> **Origin:** Consolidated from the existing D/Q review and applied revision-2 requirements; no new product decision
> **Legacy references:** Q1, Q2

## Context

Ownership, assertion and subject are different concepts. A spouse must not infer a private fact through autocomplete, counts, answers, notifications or mixed-visibility turns.

## Decision

Keep household visibility for member preferences and ordinary family/household facts, including facts about children. “For me,” “my note” and “remind me” default to private. Do not reveal private-item existence to the other adult. Explicit owner sharing uses the separate `shared` visibility under [ADR-024](adr-024.md).

## Alternatives

Private-by-default preferences were considered in Q1; household remained the accepted default. Existence-only disclosure was considered in Q2 and rejected. Converting private to household for read sharing is rejected because it loses ownership.

## Consequences

RLS and derived-data handling must protect existence as well as content. Cards show visibility. Shared rows keep an owner and grant adult reads only; no administrator takeover provides a privacy shortcut. Sensitive credentials/identifiers remain refused under the ingress boundary.

Q1/Q2 kept the existing defaults. The 100% privacy gate includes private existence, mixed turns and sharing/revocation.
