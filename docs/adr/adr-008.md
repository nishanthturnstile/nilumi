# ADR-008 — Cascaded STT → LLM → TTS (not realtime speech-to-speech) for the MVP

> **Status:** Accepted
> **Baseline:** Revision 2, reviewed 6 Oct 2026; individual decision date not recorded
> **Origin:** Extracted from Architecture §21; existing ID and accepted meaning retained
> **Legacy references:** Original ADR only

## Context

Voice capture must produce auditable transcript spans and controlled writes while preserving spoken interaction.

The speech comparison favors explicit transcription and native short clips. Realtime speech-to-speech weakens the deterministic write path, lacks keyterm biasing and costs more.

## Decision

push-to-talk sends short clips through STT, structured NLU and optional sentence TTS.

## Alternatives

Gemini Live, OpenAI Realtime.

## Consequences

transcripts for provenance, keyterm biasing, deterministic writes, lower cost; slightly higher latency, mitigated by the screen UX and streaming route-handler response.

Batch STT adds latency; measured voice and model gates remain pending.
