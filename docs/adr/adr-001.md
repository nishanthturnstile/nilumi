# ADR-001 — Channels and PWA platform

> **Decision status:** Accepted current baseline, 2026-10-10.
> **Delivery status:** selected for prototype; P0–P2. Acceptance of a decision does not establish implemented or production-validated behavior.

## Context

The voice-first PWA uses one dynamic Home canvas and persistent activities. This record owns the channels and pwa platform choice within that experience.

## Decision

Use an installed Next.js PWA on iPhone and Android with responsive desktop support. Home is the primary channel and canvas; authenticated application APIs remain channel-independent. Native, React Native and Capacitor delivery are excluded. Foreground continuing conversation is initial scope; room microphones, wake words and additional channels are deferred.

## Consequences and validation scope

Existing S1 tests validated the scoped installed-PWA capture, playback, identity, offline shell, update and push behavior on the household phones. They did not test Pipecat, continuous conversation or the new Home. Repeat those paths in P0/P2 and P8. Consolidates the platform choices formerly in ADR-015 and ADR-039.
