# ADR-036 — Operating-cost target and AI budget guardrails

> **Status:** Accepted
> **Baseline:** Revision 2, reviewed 6 Oct 2026; individual decision date not recorded
> **Origin:** Consolidated from the existing D/Q review and applied revision-2 requirements; no new product decision
> **Legacy references:** D18

## Context

Low recurring cost is an owner priority. Revision 2 makes hosting the dominant cost; estimates need measured household usage.

## Decision

Target total spend ≤ ₹3,000/month, expected ≈ ₹1,400–2,700. Keep the AI budget default at ₹800/month, separate from the expected ≈ ₹280 (≤ ₹600 with headroom) AI spend. Reserve bounded maximum provider-call cost in Postgres, reconcile actual usage, cap concurrency at two in-flight turns per member and bound retries. At 80%, use deterministic answers/evidence and cached TTS; at 100%, use the deterministic NLU grammar. Alert at each cap.

## Alternatives

A revision-1 zero-hosting-cost scenario is historical rather than the current topology. Best-effort accounting after calls would permit concurrent overspend and is rejected. Cache-hit savings are not assumed in the estimate.

## Consequences

Lists, tasks, reminders and inbox keep working at every cap. Record per-turn costs and a monthly admin view. Measure Railway in S5; revisit hosting, managed database, heavier Vault OCR or Capacitor when cost/operations warrant it. Current line-item estimates and dependency/provider prices remain in Tech Stack.

These are targets and guardrails, not a measured operating bill or a passed platform gate. Capacitor adds Apple Developer ≈ ₹8,700/year only if triggered.
