# ADR-036 — Budgets and continuous-speech cost accounting

> **Decision status:** Accepted current baseline, 2026-10-10.
> **Delivery status:** planned for MVP; P0–P8. Acceptance of a decision does not establish implemented or production-validated behavior.

## Context

The voice-first PWA uses one dynamic Home canvas and persistent activities. This record owns the budgets and continuous-speech cost accounting choice within that experience.

## Decision

Target total spend≤₹3,000/month; ceiling₹5,000/month with review above target. AI default₹800/month covers LLM, embeddings, STT and TTS; reserve worst-case bounded usage before calls/stream segments in PostgreSQL, reconcile actual costs and cap concurrent turns at two/member. At 80% prefer deterministic responses and shorter/cached speech; at 100% pause provider calls and retain manual/limited grammar functions.

Gateway team cap remainsUS$10/month; runtime US$8/month while S3 key exists. Auto top-up remains off, no additional purchase authorized. Runtime may be reconsidered at US$10 only after evaluation-key revocation. Gateway soft limits can overshoot by one call; the application ledger is the hard stop. Review balance, expiry, per-key bills, tax/card/forex and hosting monthly.

## Consequences and validation scope

Consolidates ADR-047. Old short-clip₹280/≤₹600 estimates are not current forecasts. Count continuous connected time according to actual provider billing (including silence if billed), spoken characters, generation, voice runtime/media egress and idle capacity. P0 measures sessions; P1/P8 reconcile scenarios against limits before family traffic. Historical experiment caps and failures remain in evidence, not reusable funding authorization.
