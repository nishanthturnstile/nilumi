# ADR-037 — Confirmed health facts and recorded information

> **Decision status:** Accepted current baseline, 2026-10-10.
> **Delivery status:** planned for MVP; P4. Acceptance of a decision does not establish implemented or production-validated behavior.

## Context

The voice-first PWA uses one dynamic Home canvas and persistent activities. This record owns the confirmed health facts and recorded information choice within that experience.

## Decision

Health/allergy facts including children require explicit Confirm before becoming active memory. Store asserted-by and evidence; say “based on what is recorded”. Provide recorded information, never medical advice.

## Consequences and validation scope

Pending health facts do not expire automatically but are excluded from retrieval until confirmation. Subject ambiguity needs clarification; another adult’s assertion is not clinical validation. New speech/UI paths must preserve the same boundary.
