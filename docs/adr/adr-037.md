# ADR-037 — Confirmed health facts and information-only responses

> **Status:** Accepted
> **Baseline:** Revision 2, reviewed 6 Oct 2026; individual decision date not recorded
> **Origin:** Consolidated from the existing D/Q review and applied revision-2 requirements; no new product decision
> **Legacy references:** Q10

## Context

Facts about children's health/allergies are useful from day one, while an incorrect assertion or medical advice would violate the trust model.

## Decision

Include health and allergy facts, including children's, in MVP memory only after explicit Confirm on the card. Answers qualify them as “based on what's recorded.” Provide recorded information only; never medical advice.

## Alternatives

Excluding all children's health facts was considered in Q10; the review kept inclusion with explicit confirmation. Automatic writes under the normal Undo-first flow do not apply to these facts. Classifier-driven health policy is rejected in favor of deterministic validation/schema handling.

## Consequences

Subject resolution and asserted-by provenance remain explicit; another adult's assertion is not clinical validation. Confirmation is an exception to Undo-first capture, and answerability/evidence gates still apply. Kids are subjects, not MVP users.

Q10 retained the accepted requirement; provider approval and confirmation implementation are not established by extracting it.
