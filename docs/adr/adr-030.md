# ADR-030 — Retention of domain, activity and conversational data

> **Decision status:** Accepted current baseline, 2026-10-10.
> **Delivery status:** planned for MVP; P2/P4/P7. Acceptance of a decision does not establish implemented or production-validated behavior.

## Context

The voice-first PWA uses one dynamic Home canvas and persistent activities. This record owns the retention of domain, activity and conversational data choice within that experience.

## Decision

Sources backing memories last for the memory lifetime subject to forget. No-memory source raw_text and ordinary turn bodies expire after 30 days; backing evidence is retained only with a live record. Trace text expires after 30 days; metrics last one year. No audio is stored by default; opt-in debugging lasts seven days in prototype/pilot only.

Activities and their current useful presentation remain until owner deletion or source removal, independently of transcript expiry. Archive hides from Recent without deleting domain data; explicit Delete activity erases its owned draft/presentation text. Keep only current presentation plus bounded seven-day recovery versions; source visibility/forget overrides every version immediately. Do not preserve raw transcript by copying it into a presentation.

## Consequences and validation scope

Purge jobs and export cover drafts/presentations and content references. Retention and privacy UI distinguish archive, delete activity, undo and forget. Recovery applies tombstones before serving; private generated content is not persistently cached offline.
