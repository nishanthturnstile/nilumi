# ADR-030 — Data retention and optional debug-audio capture

> **Status:** Accepted
> **Baseline:** Revision 2, reviewed 6 Oct 2026; individual decision date not recorded
> **Origin:** Consolidated from the existing D/Q review and applied revision-2 requirements; no new product decision
> **Legacy references:** Q5, Q6

## Context

Debugging and provenance require bounded retention without collecting raw household audio by default.

## Decision

Keep sources backing memories for the memory lifetime, subject to forget. Purge no-memory source `raw_text` after 30 days; purge turn bodies after 30 days unless backing a live memory card, retaining IDs. Purge trace text after 30 days and retain metrics for one year. Store no audio by default; optional debug capture lasts seven days during Phase 0 and the pilot only, and is deleted by forget.

## Alternatives

Indefinite no-memory text or always-on audio capture was not accepted. The review retained the existing 30-day purge and opt-in seven-day debug policy.

## Consequences

Retention jobs must include source, turn and trace copies. Job metadata stays only as needed for scheduling/retry/audit; push subscriptions last until revoke or 404/410. Backup retention/replay is governed by [ADR-032](adr-032.md).

Debug opt-in does not waive S0 provider eligibility or forget requirements. Q5/Q6 were default-kept resolutions, not new capture permissions.
