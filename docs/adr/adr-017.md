# ADR-017 — Provider eligibility and household acknowledgement

> **Decision status:** Accepted current baseline, 2026-10-10.
> **Delivery status:** planned for MVP; P1/P8. Acceptance of a decision does not establish implemented or production-validated behavior.

## Context

The voice-first PWA uses one dynamic Home canvas and persistent activities. This record owns the provider eligibility and household acknowledgement choice within that experience.

## Decision

Every processor (LLM, embeddings, STT, TTS, hosting, email, storage and any future extractor) needs dated eligibility, retention, training, location and configuration evidence before family data. Use approved OpenAI roles through managed Gateway routing for the restricted founding pilot; Gemini remains excluded unless eligibility changes. Sarvam speech has its own review; selection is not approval of a new streaming processor. STT receives microphone audio before final-text secret screening; disclose that boundary and provider deletion limits rather than promising audio is pre-redacted.

The owner may record acknowledgement on behalf of both adults only after explaining the current notice. Store exact current adult rows, notice version, processors, published retention, child facts/calendar content, withdrawal and deletion limits. Both adults can inspect it. New adult, processor or notice invalidates it. Either adult can withdraw; only the withdrawing adult can renew after a veto.

An inactive acknowledgement blocks all new and queued AI calls, including STT/TTS and active-session renewal. Close active provider streams and stop buffered output promptly on withdrawal; do not promise recall of already transmitted bytes. Manual lists/tasks/reminders/Inbox remain available. New calendar linking is refused; an existing eligible read-only connection may sync for display without sending text to AI.

Before external households, helpers, child access or commercial use, require a ZDR-capable approved route, ZDR on every LLM/embedding call, dated per-model proof and routing receipts, explicit negative-policy tests, minimization review and primary legal verification. Speech processors retain separate eligibility gates. A paid Vercel platform plan is not authorized by the credits exception.

## Consequences and validation scope

Consolidates applicable ADR-046, ADR-052 and ADR-054 policy. Existing acknowledgement belongs to the spike environment and is not copied as production approval. S-VGW documentary retention evidence is restricted-pilot evidence, not direct observation or ZDR proof.
