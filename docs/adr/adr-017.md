# ADR-017 — AI provider eligibility is a release gate; default LLM roles start with OpenAI unless terms force a change

> **Status:** Accepted
> **Baseline:** Revision 2, reviewed 6 Oct 2026; individual decision date not recorded
> **Origin:** Extracted from Architecture §21; existing ID and accepted meaning retained
> **Legacy references:** Original ADR only

## Context

An adult-operated household app stores facts about children; account eligibility and processor retention cannot be inferred from paid/no-training labels.

The STT review exposes consumer and age restrictions, uncertain retention and approval gaps. Provider eligibility must precede comparisons with family clips; local forget cannot erase provider-side copies.

## Decision

every provider (LLM, embedding, STT, TTS, extractor, hosting, email and storage) passes S0 eligibility and data-terms checks before family data flows. The default `nlu`/`answer` role starts with OpenAI and `embed` with the approved embedding model, with Anthropic as challenger; Gemini is excluded unless eligibility changes.

## Alternatives

choose purely by benchmark/cost; use unverified consumer terms.

## Consequences

provider choice rests on eligibility and data terms first, then accuracy and latency.

S0 remains pending for the listed processors, including hosted extractors before H1. Record effective settings and re-check yearly and before kid mode. Default LLM model names are hypotheses until the bake-offs.
