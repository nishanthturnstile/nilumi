# ADR-054 — Withdrawing the household acknowledgement stops every AI provider call

> **Status:** Accepted
> **Baseline:** October 10, 2026
> **Origin:** Owner decision on application-flow open question OQ-3 ([28 Application Flows §9.2](../core/28-application-flows.md#92-resolved-questions))
> **Legacy references:** None
> **Supersedes / superseded by:** Amends [ADR-046](adr-046.md) (scope of the acknowledgement's effect). Aligns Architecture with the existing [Roadmap Phase 1](../core/05-implementation-roadmap.md#phase-1--walking-skeleton-with-safety-rails) wording ("withdrawing it stops every AI call")

## Context

[ADR-046](adr-046.md) made the household acknowledgement gate every language-model and embedding call, while speech-to-text (STT) and text-to-speech (TTS) stayed direct and governed by their own provider rows. The Roadmap said withdrawal "stops every AI call". The two readings differed on whether voice keeps working after an adult withdraws. Audio and reply text also leave the household for an AI provider (Sarvam today), so an adult who withdraws reasonably expects that to stop too.

## Decision

- While the household acknowledgement is not active (never recorded, withdrawn, outdated notice, or changed adults), Nilumi makes **no AI provider call of any kind**: language models, embeddings, speech-to-text and text-to-speech. This includes the optional Today summary and any queued or background job; each checks again immediately before sending.
- Calls already sent when the adult withdraws can't be recalled; nothing further is sent.
- The household enters hard-cap degraded mode. Typed text works in the fixed command set; voice input and spoken replies are paused with a visible reason. Lists, tasks, reminders and the inbox keep working.
- Calendar behavior is unchanged from [ADR-046](adr-046.md): new links are refused; an existing link keeps syncing events for display because Google is not an AI provider, and no calendar text reaches a model.
- The acknowledgement notice names every AI processor, including the speech provider.

## Alternatives

- **Gate only language-model and embedding calls (ADR-046 as written).** Rejected by the owner: withdrawing should stop household audio and text leaving for any AI provider.
- **Also stop non-AI processors (email, push, hosting).** Rejected: sign-in, reminders and storage would stop working, and these processors don't run AI over family content.

## Consequences

- Voice is unavailable while the acknowledgement is inactive, including before it is first recorded. Phase 1 voice turns therefore start only after the owner records it in the new app.
- The STT and TTS adapters need the same fail-closed check as the gateway wrapper, with tests for withdrawal, outdated notices and job-time rechecks.
- Cached template audio isn't a provider call, but replies stay silent while paused to keep the behavior simple to explain.
