# ADR-034 — Defer household-help access to H6

> **Status:** Accepted
> **Baseline:** Revision 2, reviewed 6 Oct 2026; individual decision date not recorded
> **Origin:** Consolidated from the existing D/Q review and applied revision-2 requirements; no new product decision
> **Legacy references:** D17, Q9

## Context

The MVP is one household used by two adults. External list access needs a narrower role and renewed privacy planning.

## Decision

Keep shopping-list access within the household for the MVP. Defer cook/household-help access to the shopping-list-only H6 horizon.

## Alternatives

Immediate external sharing or a broad guest memory role was not accepted. The review kept the existing later-horizon default.

## Consequences

Do not add an MVP external-user role. External contacts remain entities rather than users. H6 sequencing depends on pilot evidence and preserves the other horizon IDs.

D17 and Q9 resolve to this one record; H6 is not a completed capability or a new MVP commitment.
