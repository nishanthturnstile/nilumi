# ADR-024 — Explicit, owner-only memory sharing (`shared` visibility)

> **Status:** Accepted
> **Baseline:** Revision 2, reviewed 6 Oct 2026; individual decision date not recorded
> **Origin:** Extracted from Architecture §21; existing ID and accepted meaning retained
> **Legacy references:** D2

## Context

Private facts sometimes need adult read sharing without surrendering the original owner's edit, forget and un-share rights.

Converting a private row to household would clear ownership. Duplication creates divergent copies and unsafe revocation. Sharing also needs safe private-entity projections, visibility-aware history and derived-card revocation.

## Decision

private memories can become `shared` only through an explicit share cue or owner action. Shared rows remain owner-owned, readable by adults, not children, and owner-only for edit/forget/un-share.

## Alternatives

convert private to household; duplicate into spouse-visible private rows; app-layer ACLs.

## Consequences

ownership is preserved, un-share is possible, cardinality-one household slots are not silently overwritten, safe entity projections and new privacy evals are required.

Sharing is not a correction or promotion into a household cardinality-one slot. Revocation uses [ADR-033](adr-033.md); it cannot undo what the other adult already saw.
