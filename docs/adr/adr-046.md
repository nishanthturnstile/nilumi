# ADR-046 — Stay on Vercel AI Gateway with purchased credits during validation; ZDR becomes a production gate

> **Status:** Accepted
> **Baseline:** October 8, 2026
> **Origin:** Owner decision on October 8: Cloudflare was evaluated and not chosen; buy Vercel AI Gateway credits only, with no paid Vercel plan; zero data retention is not required while the founding household validates the product; production needs no-training, ZDR and no sensitive data sent to models
> **Owner update (October 8, after purchase):** US$20 of credits bought (owner-reported); about US$10 a month in total for every key; the owner records the household acknowledgement on behalf of both adults; S3 gets a cumulative US$2.00 cap on its own key under [ADR-040](adr-040.md). The acknowledgement and spending sections below reflect this update.
> **Legacy references:** None
> **Supersedes / superseded by:** Partially supersedes [ADR-038](adr-038.md) (ZDR timing and the team-wide allowlist). Amends [ADR-040](adr-040.md) (credit funding) and [ADR-021](adr-021.md) (one scoped metered exception).

## Context

[ADR-038](adr-038.md) routes LLM and embedding calls through Vercel AI Gateway
with team-wide zero data retention (ZDR), no-training enforcement and a
provider allowlist. The [October 7 verification](../08-s3-gateway-verification.md)
found that the team is on Hobby. On Hobby, ZDR and dashboard allowlists are
unavailable, so family data stayed blocked.

On October 8, [Cloudflare AI Gateway](../04-research.md#cloudflare-ai-gateway-primary-docs-checked-8-oct-2026)
was evaluated and not chosen:
- Its ZDR covers only catalog-marked models.
- Its credits carry a 5% fee.
- It would add another processor.

The owner then decided that the founding household's pilot is validation, not
production. Its data may be processed without ZDR, but no request may be used
for training.

[Vercel's documentation](../04-research.md#vercel-ai-gateway-credits-and-controls-checked-8-oct-2026)
(checked 8 Oct 2026) states the following.

**Pricing and credits**
- Tokens are charged at provider list price with no markup.
- Payment processing fees are the customer's.
- Buying credits moves the team to the paid credit tier and ends the US$5 monthly free credit.
- Auto top-up is off by default.
- The purchase dialog in the dashboard says credits expire one year after purchase.

**Privacy controls**
- `disallowPromptTraining` is free and set per request. It applies to fallbacks and fails with `no_providers_available` when no compliant provider exists.
- It does not apply to BYOK requests (bring-your-own provider keys). By default, the Gateway does not route by training policy.
- ZDR is available on Pro and Enterprise only.
- The `only` filter restricts routing to the listed providers. `order` is just a preference: providers not listed can still serve the request.

**Budgets**
- Budgets can be set per team, project, API key or user. Each refreshes `daily`, `weekly`, `monthly` (UTC midnight on the 1st) or `none`, which never resets and is cumulative.
- A budget is a soft cap: the request that crosses the limit completes, and later requests are rejected with HTTP 402 `quota_for_entity_exceeded`.
- Editing a budget keeps the spend already counted in the period; deleting and re-creating it starts counting from zero.
- Alerts at 50, 75 and 100% fire for custom budgets only, at most once per period; key-budget alerts go to the key's creator.
- Project budgets apply only to that project's OIDC tokens; API-key spend is never attributed to a project.
- A budget rejection can surface in AI SDK 7 as `GatewayInternalServerError`.
- No low-balance alert is documented; only auto top-up watches the balance.

**Fair use**
- Hobby is for non-commercial personal use.

## Decision

**Route.** LLM and embedding calls continue through Vercel AI Gateway on Hobby, using
Gateway-managed credentials funded by purchased credits.
- No paid Vercel plan.
- No bring-your-own-key (BYOK) route: no team BYOK credentials and no request-scoped keys.
- Speech-to-text (STT) and text-to-speech (TTS) stay direct and keep their own [Tech Stack §7](../03-tech-stack.md#7-provider-eligibility-and-data-policies-release-gate) rows.

**Pilot controls on every call, from day one.** These replace ADR-038's ZDR and
dashboard-allowlist conditions until the production gate.

1. **One wrapper.** A single gateway wrapper behind [ADR-022](adr-022.md)'s role registry builds every LLM and embedding model. It sets:
   - `disallowPromptTraining: true`
   - an `only` list of approved providers for each role, with `order` used only as a preference inside that list
   - `store: false` on endpoints that support it

   A CI test fails if any other code builds a Gateway model or calls a provider SDK for these roles.
2. **Routing receipts.** Each response's routing metadata is checked. An unknown, BYOK or out-of-list provider stops further calls for that role until reviewed, as in [ADR-040](adr-040.md).
3. **Per-model records.** `config/providers.md` records for each approved model and embedding model:
   - no-training status
   - published retention, including abuse monitoring
   - where `store: false` applies
   - fallback eligibility

   A fallback must meet the same bar. If no no-training embedding route is verified, retrieval uses full-text and trigram search.
4. **Sensitive-input minimisation stays mandatory.** The scanner covers voice, text, imports and background inputs. It refuses or masks credentials, financial and government identifiers, OTPs and security codes. It is heuristic, and Settings → Privacy says so.

**Household acknowledgement.** Before the first real model call or calendar link,
the owner records one plain-language acknowledgement on behalf of both adults,
confirming that he has explained it to the other adult. It covers:
- which processors receive data
- their published retention terms (no fixed maximum is promised)
- that facts about the children, and calendar titles and locations, are included
- how to withdraw
- that deleting in Nilumi cannot erase copies a provider keeps

Rules:
- **Visible to every adult.** Settings → Privacy shows every adult the same notice, who recorded it, when, and the notice version.
- **Covers exactly the current adults.** The covered adults are stored as rows, not inferred. The household counts as acknowledged only while the active acknowledgement matches the current notice version and covers exactly the household's current adults. A new notice version, a new processor or a new adult member supersedes it, and a new acknowledgement must cover them.
- **Either adult can withdraw, and withdrawal is a veto.** After a withdrawal, only the adult who withdrew can record the next acknowledgement; the owner cannot override it.
- **Effect of no active acknowledgement.** No new or queued LLM or embedding call leaves the wrapper (jobs re-check when they run), and the household is in hard-cap degraded mode. New calendar links are refused; an existing link keeps syncing events for display, but no calendar text reaches a model. Lists, tasks, reminders and the inbox keep working.
- **Fail closed.** The gateway wrapper performs this check before every LLM and embedding call ([Architecture §15.4](../02-architecture.md#154-data-minimization-and-provider-retention)).

**Spending.** The owner bought US$20 of credits and budgets about **US$10 a
month in total** for every Gateway key. The Postgres reservation ledger stays the
hard stop ([ADR-036](adr-036.md), [ADR-047](adr-047.md)). Gateway budgets are
soft-cap backstops that can overshoot by one request:

| Budget | Limit | Refresh | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Team | US$10 | Monthly | Outer cap across every key; alerts at 50, 75 and 100% |
| Runtime key (team-owned) | US$8 | Monthly | Railway runtime; alerts at 50, 75 and 100% |
| S3 evaluation key (team-owned) | The S3 allowance remaining when it is set: US$2.00 cumulative owner cap minus the S3 ledger total (US$1.722693540 after synthetic correctness acceptance; the runner still enforces US$0.50 with US$0.222693540 remaining until migration) | None | S3 only, under [ADR-040](adr-040.md); the runner's durable ledger is the hard stop |
| S-VGW canary key | About US$0.10 | None | S-VGW proofs only; revoked afterwards |

- **Runtime key.** US$8 is about ₹696 at ₹87 per US$1, before card and foreign-exchange charges. It is US$10 minus the S3 and canary allowances, rounded down; the nominal total is about US$9.98 before any one-request overshoot. It stays at US$8 even in months when S3 spends nothing. It is no longer looser than the ₹800 AI ledger, which also funds STT and TTS. It trips first only if Gateway spend alone passes about ₹696, which already points to a fault, and the result is the same hard-cap degraded mode. Once the S3 key is revoked, the runtime key may rise to US$10.
- **Credits.** Auto top-up stays off. The owner checks the balance, spend per key and the earliest credit expiry once a month, and tops up manually when the balance falls below one month's team budget.
- **Failures.** Budget (HTTP 402 `quota_for_entity_exceeded`), credit-exhaustion and `no_providers_available` errors are terminal: no retry and no challenger fallback. They enter hard-cap degraded mode.

**Gate S-VGW, before household runtime traffic.** S-VGW uses synthetic canary
prompts only, under its own small cap; it is not an S3 smoke. It proves:
- Credits are purchased (US$20, owner-reported on October 8), auto top-up is off, and the team has no BYOK credentials.
- The team, runtime, evaluation and canary budgets match the table above, and the keys belong to the team.
- Budget exhaustion fails closed, with the AI SDK 7 error mapped to degraded mode. The proof lowers the canary key's limit to its current spend (editing keeps the counted spend) and expects HTTP 402 on the next request.
- `disallowPromptTraining` and `only` hold on chat, embedding and fallback paths.
- A request restricted to a non-compliant provider fails.
- `store: false` reaches the provider with the pinned SDK where it is supported.
- Routing receipts are recorded.

Evidence goes in [doc 08](../08-s3-gateway-verification.md). When S-VGW passes and the
household acknowledgement is recorded, the gateway rows in [Tech Stack §7](../03-tech-stack.md#7-provider-eligibility-and-data-policies-release-gate)
become **Green — founding-household pilot only**.

**Founding household.** The owner's own household: its adults as users and its
children as subjects only.

**Production privacy gate.** It must pass before any of these:
- a user outside the founding household, including relatives elsewhere
- helper access (H6)
- kid mode (H5)
- a second household ([ADR-048](adr-048.md))
- any commercial use

It requires all of the following:
1. **Route.** Vercel Pro or Enterprise, or another ZDR-capable route chosen in a new ADR with its cost. Pro is a platform subscription and is not covered by this decision's exception.
2. **ZDR on every call.** ZDR on every LLM and embedding request, per request or team-wide, with dated per-model records.
3. **Approved routing.** An `only` list for every role, negative tests for non-compliant routes, and failure without bypass when no compliant route exists.
4. **Verifiable in tests.** ZDR is asserted on routing receipts in tests and in production monitoring.
5. **Minimisation.** Sensitive-data minimisation is reviewed against real pilot inputs.
6. **Legal.** The [ADR-048](adr-048.md) legal check passes.
7. **Evidence.** Contract and configuration evidence is dated.

STT and TTS processors pass their own gates. The H1 Vault processor gate
([ADR-026](adr-026.md)) decides separately whether document text needs ZDR even
inside the founding household.

**ADR-021 exception.** Vercel AI Gateway credits are the only metered Vercel
service allowed. Hosting, Workflow, Cron and other platform services remain
excluded.

**Not authorized.** This decision does not authorize any hosted S3 call or
enabling the staging evaluator. Each S3 round needs its own owner authorization
under [ADR-040](adr-040.md).

## Alternatives

- **Cloudflare AI Gateway with Unified Billing.** Rejected for household traffic: catalog-limited ZDR, a 5% credit fee and a new processor. The S3 spike keeps an explicitly selected, unfunded Cloudflare adapter for synthetic tests only ([doc 09](../09-s3-cloudflare-gateway.md)).
- **Vercel Pro now.** Deferred: a fixed monthly subscription before validation. It is one route through the production gate.
- **OpenRouter.** Rejected: its fees and retention terms are unclear.
- **Direct provider SDKs.** Rejected: there is no single enforcement point.
- **Self-hosted LiteLLM or Bifrost.** Rejected: another service to run.
- **Keep family data blocked until ZDR.** Rejected by the owner: it delays validation with real use.

## Consequences

**Benefits**
- No new processor and no token markup.
- The existing integration and S3 evidence carry over.
- The same wrapper gains ZDR at the production gate by adding the per-request flag on a ZDR-capable plan.

**Costs and risks**
- **Provider retention during the pilot.** Providers may keep prompts for abuse monitoring or legal reasons under their published terms, and Nilumi cannot erase those copies.
- **Fair use.** Hobby's non-commercial rule limits use to the founding household. Any wider use triggers the production gate.
- **Card and forex fees.** These, and the lost free credit, count toward cost ([ADR-047](adr-047.md)).
- **Regression.** Dropping the no-training flag or the `only` list, a BYOK credential appearing, or an unchecked routing receipt fails the privacy gate.
