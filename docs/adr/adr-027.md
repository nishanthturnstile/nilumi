# ADR-027 — Voice reply defaults and modality matching

> **Status:** Accepted
> **Baseline:** Revision 2, reviewed 6 Oct 2026; individual decision date not recorded
> **Origin:** Consolidated from the existing D/Q review and applied revision-2 requirements; no new product decision
> **Legacy references:** D3, Q3

## Context

Voice is the primary interaction, but typing in a meeting or at night must not make the phone speak.

## Decision

Keep `auto` as the default: voice input receives a short spoken reply plus on-screen text/cards; text input receives text only. Keep `always` and `never` settings. The screen is always updated.

## Alternatives

Always speaking risks interrupting quiet use; text-only defaults lose the voice-first interaction. Both remain explicit settings rather than the default.

## Consequences

The Talk screen leads with hold-to-talk and keeps text available. Speech confirms while the screen explains; per-sentence validated TTS and playback fallback remain Architecture contracts.

Voice selection and first-audio p95 ≤ 700 ms remain S4 checks; installed-phone playback remains S1.
