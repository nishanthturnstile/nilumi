# ADR-047 — Pilot cost allowance up to ₹5,000/month

> **Status:** Accepted
> **Baseline:** October 8, 2026
> **Origin:** Owner decision during the October 8 planning review
> **Legacy references:** None
> **Supersedes / superseded by:** Amends [ADR-036](adr-036.md); ADR-036's target, AI budget default and guardrails remain

## Context

[ADR-036](adr-036.md) targets total spend of at most ₹3,000/month. The
expansion adds several smaller costs:
- one optional LLM summary per member per day for the brief
- bounded agent runs after the pilot
- card and foreign-exchange charges on prepaid Vercel AI Gateway credits, and the end of the US$5 monthly free credit once credits are bought ([ADR-046](adr-046.md))
- some measurement uncertainty in Railway costs

The owner accepted a higher ceiling for the pilot rather than cutting scope.
The ceiling does not cover a Vercel Pro or Enterprise subscription; that needs
its own decision at the production privacy gate ([ADR-046](adr-046.md)).

## Decision

**Ceiling.** The pilot may spend up to **₹5,000/month** in total.
- This is a ceiling, not a plan; the target stays at most ₹3,000/month.
- The ₹800/month AI budget default and its 80% and 100% degradation steps are unchanged.
- The S3 testing cap under [ADR-040](adr-040.md) is separate and unchanged.
- Spending above ₹3,000 needs the owner's review of the month's cost report.

**Reservation.** The maximum-cost reservation in Postgres covers:
- every model call, including brief summaries and each agent step
- embeddings
- retries and fallbacks

A reservation that times out stays charged until reconciled.

**Gateway backstops.** The Vercel API-key budgets, alerts and credit balance in
[ADR-046](adr-046.md) are backstops, not the hard stop:
- The team budget is US$10/month across every key. The runtime key's budget is US$8/month (≈₹696 at ₹87 per US$1) while the S3 key exists, and may rise to US$10 once it is revoked. At US$8 it is no longer looser than the ₹800 ledger, which also funds STT and TTS: it trips first only if Gateway spend alone passes about ₹696, which already points to a fault, and the result is the same hard-cap degraded mode.
- Vercel documents no low-balance alert, so the owner checks the balance, per-key spend and credit expiry monthly and tops up manually.
- Gateway alerts at 50, 75 and 100% are separate from the app's 80% and 100% degradation steps.
- The Gateway month starts at UTC midnight on the 1st, so it can differ from the ledger month.

**Accounting.** Cost reports separate three things:
- **Prepaid credit purchases**, including card and foreign-exchange charges. These are cash out.
- **Unused balance.** Credits expire one year after purchase.
- **Consumption**, which counts toward monthly spend.

Losing the US$5 free credit is not a fee, but the report shows it.

**Agent runs.** Each bounded run type declares a worst-case cost. A run that
cannot reserve that amount does not start.

## Alternatives

- **Keep ₹3,000 as a hard ceiling.** Rejected by the owner: it would force scope cuts before usage is measured.
- **Have no ceiling during the pilot.** Rejected: it would remove spending discipline.

## Consequences

The monthly admin view shows target, ceiling and actual spend separately. Tech
Stack §5 gains cost lines for:
- Vercel AI Gateway credit consumption
- card and forex charges
- the brief summary
- agent steps

Spending is reviewed again at pilot exit.
