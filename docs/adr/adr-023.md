# ADR-023 — Email-code sign-in with optional WebAuthn step-up

> **Status:** Accepted
> **Baseline:** Revision 2, reviewed 6 Oct 2026; individual decision date not recorded
> **Origin:** Extracted from Architecture §21; existing ID and accepted meaning retained
> **Legacy references:** D13

## Context

Sign-in and lost-phone recovery must work inside the installed iPhone app without help from the other adult.

Email codes avoid Safari handoff and popup problems of magic links, QR enrolment and OAuth popups. Passwords still need email reset; SMS/WhatsApp add regulatory/business setup. Primary passkeys and printed recovery material add household ceremony. WebKit session resets remain a watch item.

## Decision

Better Auth email OTP + multi-session is the primary sign-in/recovery flow; optional platform WebAuthn is only a step-up for export, private views after inactivity, forget-all and possibly Vault originals.

## Alternatives

primary WebAuthn, magic links, Google sign-in, invite links/QR, passwords, SMS/WhatsApp OTP.

## Consequences

one mechanism works inside the installed iPhone app for first sign-in, new devices and recovery; mailbox takeover is account takeover and is stated honestly; Resend becomes a processor.

Use invite-only profiles, 90-day sliding HttpOnly sessions and self-service revoke. Primary passkeys, recovery codes, second authenticator, enrolment tokens and break-glass are removed. No admin takeover of another adult after first sign-in. S1 verifies sessions; the version floor and advisories remain in Tech Stack/research. Mandatory biometric access to Vault originals remains an H1 decision.
