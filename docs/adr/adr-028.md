# ADR-028 — Nilumi product name and future wake phrase

> **Status:** Accepted
> **Baseline:** Revision 2, reviewed 6 Oct 2026; individual decision date not recorded
> **Origin:** Consolidated from the existing D/Q review and applied revision-2 requirements; no new product decision
> **Legacy references:** D4, Q7

## Context

The name should be warm, calm, easy for Indian-English/Tamil speakers and children, and distinct enough for a future wake word. Twenty-five names were screened.

## Decision

Use **Nilumi**, pronounced `NI-lu-mi` / நிலுமி, coined from Tamil *nila* (moon) plus light. Reserve **“Hey Nilumi”** for the future H2 room speaker. The MVP stays hold-to-talk.

## Alternatives

Aayuli was the runner-up but is vowel-heavy and can blur with “Hey Julie.” Kuralo, Kivani and Nenavo had domain or conflict concerns. The shortlist and dated RDAP/DNS findings stay in research.

## Consequences

Use consistent branding. Custom openWakeWord/microWakeWord training with Indian-English synthetic TTS and household negatives is future H2 work; Porcupine remains a licensed option per the review. A formal Indian/WIPO trademark search precedes public use; nothing was registered during research.

The domain decision and purchase prerequisite remain in [ADR-016](adr-016.md); historical availability does not establish registration or trademark clearance.
