# ADR-028 — Nilumi name and Home conversation entry

> **Decision status:** Accepted current baseline, 2026-10-10.
> **Delivery status:** planned for MVP; P0/P2. Acceptance of a decision does not establish implemented or production-validated behavior.

## Context

The voice-first PWA uses one dynamic Home canvas and persistent activities. This record owns the nilumi name and home conversation entry choice within that experience.

## Decision

Use Nilumi, pronounced NI-lu-mi / நிலுமி. Home has the persistent voice/text composer and listening/speaking indicators. Reserve “Hey Nilumi” for a deferred room device; the PWA starts a foreground conversation by tap.

## Consequences and validation scope

Historical naming/domain checks do not establish registration or trademark clearance. Trademark verification precedes public branding expansion. No wake-word training is required for P0–P8.
