# ADR-025 — No classifier model in the MVP; instrumented experiments and revisit triggers

> **Status:** Accepted
> **Baseline:** Revision 2, reviewed 6 Oct 2026; individual decision date not recorded
> **Origin:** Extracted from Architecture §21; existing ID and accepted meaning retained
> **Legacy references:** D12

## Context

A classifier might improve intent routing or secret recall, but a new runtime or misroute can undermine a small household pipeline.

Recent reviewed releases are safety/PII classifiers, not intent routers, with no Tanglish evidence. Pre-NLU routing saves very little and duplicates NLU; possible secrets cannot be sent to a third-party detector. Detailed insertion-point comparisons remain in research.

## Decision

no classifier is in the production turn path for MVP. Two offline experiments run in `evals/`: embedding-kNN intent routing vs the LLM intent, and PII recall with PII-Tracer / Qwen3Guard against the deterministic detector.

## Alternatives

pre-NLU intent classifier, safety/PII classifier in production, health/visibility classifiers.

## Consequences

no extra runtime/service or false-routing risk now; revisit triggers are explicit; document-type classification arrives with the Vault and safety classification with kid mode.

Experiments are planned, not completed. Revisit if prompt-size-driven NLU p95 > 2 s, LLM spend > $5/month, simple-command intent accuracy < 90%, or secret recall < 100%. H1/H5 classifiers remain later.
