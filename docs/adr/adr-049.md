# ADR-049 — No WhatsApp channel; work alongside WhatsApp instead

> **Status:** Accepted
> **Baseline:** October 8, 2026
> **Origin:** Owner decision during the October 8 planning review (WhatsApp as a future adapter only), changed to rejection after research found Meta's AI-provider ban
> **Legacy references:** None
> **Supersedes / superseded by:** None. Channel adapters remain possible under [ADR-001](adr-001.md)

## Context

Indian families coordinate in WhatsApp groups, so a WhatsApp channel looked
attractive. The [October 8 review](../04-research.md#platform-constraints)
found that, from 15 Jan 2026, Meta's WhatsApp Business Solution Terms prohibit
general-purpose AI assistants whose primary function is AI. Removals of large
AI bots have been reported. This is secondary evidence; India per-message rates
and any competition-law action were not verified.

## Decision

Nilumi does not build a WhatsApp channel.

**Delivery channels, in order of preference:**
1. Web Push in the PWA ([ADR-035](adr-035.md)), already in use
2. A Telegram bot adapter as a post-pilot horizon
3. SMS only for critical items, if ever needed
4. RCS later

**Positioning.** Nilumi works alongside WhatsApp, not inside it.

**Revisit triggers:**
- Meta changes the terms.
- A primary reading of the terms confirms that a permitted use fits Nilumi.

## Alternatives

- **Build a WhatsApp Business adapter.** Rejected: it breaches the terms, risks removal, and puts family text with another processor.
- **Use unofficial WhatsApp libraries.** Rejected: they breach the terms and risk account bans.
- **Import text forwarded into Nilumi.** A separate candidate, not part of this decision. Android can use the Web Share Target API in the installed PWA. iPhone has no share sheet into a PWA: pasting into Talk is the baseline, an iOS Shortcut posting to a dedicated capture endpoint is an option, and a native share extension exists only under the Capacitor contingency ([ADR-015](adr-015.md)). Forwarded text is untrusted data ([Architecture §20.9](../02-architecture.md#209-agentic-horizon)).

## Consequences

No WhatsApp build, cost or processor review is needed. Habits form through the
PWA and push. The Today screen must remain useful without push.
