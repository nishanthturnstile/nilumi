# ADR-011 — graphile-worker for jobs and reminders

> **Status:** Accepted
> **Baseline:** Revision 2, reviewed 6 Oct 2026; individual decision date not recorded
> **Origin:** Extracted from Architecture §21; existing ID and accepted meaning retained
> **Legacy references:** D8

## Context

Reminder scheduling, business writes, maintenance and recovery jobs need one transactional authority.

Postgres-native jobs fit always-on Railway without another vendor. pg-boss is plausible but less integrated into the existing baseline; BullMQ needs Redis; hosted workflows add vendors, fees or platform dependence. DBOS remains a later alternative if durable TypeScript needs outgrow these jobs.

## Decision

run graphile-worker in the Railway `worker` service for reminders, maintenance, forget-journal append, embeddings backfill and backup/restore tests.

## Alternatives

pg-boss, BullMQ, cron-only, hosted durable workflows.

## Consequences

jobs live in the same Postgres authority as business data, occurrence enqueue is transactional through `schedule_occurrence`, and delivery is at-least-once with idempotent claims. History: this was briefly replaced by Vercel Workflow in revision 1 and restored when hosting moved to Railway.

S5 is a hard gate: ≥ 200 occurrences with edits, quiet hours and restarts, all dispatched within 60 s with no stale sends. Phase 5 repeats it against the implemented feature.
