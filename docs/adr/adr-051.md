# ADR-051 — Bound live S-VGW quota verification at US$1.05 total

> **Status:** Accepted
> **Baseline:** October 10, 2026
> **Origin:** Owner approval: “Approve US$1.05 total for the live quota test”
> **Supersedes / superseded by:** Amends ADR-050's aggregate S-VGW allowance and ADR-046's unsupported quota-lowering method; other release gates remain required

The total conservative S-VGW allowance is **US$1.05 across all rounds**, including
the closed run's US$0.001063. Preserve its ledger, halted state and failed report.
A new key does not reset the allowance. Runtime/evaluation/team limits stay as
recorded; no credit purchase, auto-reload or family traffic is enabled.

Vercel's minimum US$1 soft budget prevents the original tiny-spend lowering
method. A fresh isolated US$1 cumulative canary key may accumulate bounded
synthetic usage until the budget is exhausted, then make one tiny request to
verify HTTP 402 `quota_for_entity_exceeded`. The crossing request must fit a
US$0.01 reservation and the US$1.05 aggregate ceiling. Refresh account readbacks,
metering and catalog prices; reserve before every dispatch. Unknown costs remain
charged conservatively. No retry, fallback, BYOK or team-credit exhaustion.

Resolve the explicit `no_providers_available` policy proof before quota warm-up.
An unexpected response stops the round. Diagnostics reserve at most US$0.005;
the warm-up's frozen fixture, attempt bounds and exact payload must be tested
before execution. Run shutdown and used-key revocation verification on every exit.
This approval authorizes the bounded live quota test; it neither waives a proof
nor guarantees that the provider will produce the required outcomes.

The implementation uses a separate aggregate ledger with preserved-source
provenance. The first diagnostic stage retains the existing version-3 child
runner and its US$1 cap, leaving its historical format unchanged. Quota warm-up
is enabled only after its additional manifest/preflight checks are implemented.

See [the completion sequence](../plans/26-s-vgw-completion-plan.md#proposed-sequence-to-close-s-vgw)
and [ADR-050](adr-050.md). S-VGW and durable household acknowledgement remain
separate gates before the first family LLM/embedding call.
