# ADR-048 — Family-first contracts with future household isolation

> **Decision status:** Accepted current baseline, 2026-10-10.
> **Delivery status:** planned for MVP; P1. Acceptance of a decision does not establish implemented or production-validated behavior.

## Context

The voice-first PWA uses one dynamic Home canvas and persistent activities. This record owns the family-first contracts with future household isolation choice within that experience.

## Decision

Use household_id on all owned data and composite foreign keys; session determines household. Admin is an adult capability, never a privacy bypass. Keep family relationships as data. Tests include another synthetic household even though only the founding family is served.

## Consequences and validation scope

Resource grants, delegations and consents remain deferred schema designs. Primary legal verification and production privacy gate precede any external household regardless of date. This record does not claim legal exemption review is complete.
